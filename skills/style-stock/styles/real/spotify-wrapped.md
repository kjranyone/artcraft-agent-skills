# REAL STOCK: Spotify Wrapped (2016-2024 annual campaign)

**Work**: Spotify Wrapped — Spotify's annual personalized year-in-review
campaign, one of the most shared brand campaigns of the streaming era.
Design by Spotify in-house + collabs; art direction famous for loud
color-block duotones, oversized numerals, personality copy, and strict
beat-synced kinetic type.
**Provenance**: ⚠️ decomposed from the campaign's public design language and
widely published analyses; shot-level scrub not performed. Confidence: high
on tokens/grammar, medium on exact timings.

**Why this work matters**: it made **oversized numerals + data-as-copy +
color blocks** a global visual language, and proved that strict beat-sync +
bold flat color can carry a whole campaign without any photography.

## Decomposition

**Structure (each story card, 3-5 s)**: full-frame color block (duotone
paired color scheme, changes every card) → oversized numeral or stat slams
in → personality copy line lands under it → next card CUTS (no dissolve) on
the beat.

**Tokens**:
- color blocks: loud paired duotones, e.g. `#1db954`+`#191414`,
  `#503750`+`#ffc864`, `#0d72ea`+`#f573a0` — two per card, swapped next card
- numeral: **the biggest element**, 55-70% of frame height, type color
  contrasts the block
- copy: 1 short line, personality voice ("You were in the top 1%."), 40-48
  px equivalent
- NO photography, NO logos in the middle cards — logo only on the first and
  last card

**The grammar**:
1. One card = one stat = one color block. Cut on the beat, never dissolve.
2. The numeral lands FIRST (with a 2-3 frame overshoot), copy follows 0.3 s
   later.
3. Color pairs rotate; the palette stays saturated and flat (no gradients
   inside blocks).
4. Personality copy > precision copy ("top 1%" beats "top 0.87%").

## EffectCraft recipe

- Color blocks: full-frame solids, duotone pair rotation.
- Numerals: Big Shoulders Bold 500-700 px equivalents, overshoot 115→98→100.
- Cut: hard, on beat; optionally 1-frame white flash at 12% on the two
  biggest beats.
- Copy: Noto Sans JP Bold, pops 0.3 s after the numeral, exits with it.

## Anti-patterns

- Dissolves between cards (must CUT).
- Gradients inside color blocks; photography; more than one stat per card.
- Small numerals (if you can read it comfortably, it is too small).

## Best for

annual recaps, stats/milestones (2,000社 → perfect), music-adjacent youth
brand, shareable social cuts.
