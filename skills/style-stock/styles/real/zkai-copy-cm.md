# REAL STOCK: Z会「わたしたちをこえてゆけ」(2009) — JP copy-CM masterpiece

**Work**: Z会 (correspondence education) brand CM series, dir. known for
poetic copy + typography; the series 「わたしたちをこえてゆけ」 (2009) is the
celebrated entry, with the 「手紙」 series (Southern All Stars「涙のキッス」)
also iconic. Copywriter-led, ACC-recognized.
**Provenance**: ⚠️ decomposed from industry articles (金森マーケティング
オフィス analysis; pixiv/wiki summaries) + general knowledge. Shot-level
scrub not performed — treat timings as grammar, not timecode.

**Why this work matters**: THE reference for "copy as the only visual" in
Japan. A child's voice reads lines of copy that appear one by one on a plain
field. No product, no faces, no motion design — restraint as spectacle. It
proves: a 30 s film can be **copy + field + voice + one piano**, and win
grand-prix awards.

## Decomposition

**Structure (30 s)**: white/paper field throughout → copy lines appear one
by one (6-8 lines total), each holding 3-4 s → child voice reads each line
0.5 s after it appears → final line holds → logo + company name, 2 s.

**Tokens**:
- field: paper white `#f7f5f0` (or deep sumi for the 「手紙」 variant)
- copy: sumi `#1a1a1e`, mincho (明朝) class, 72-90 px, tracking +4
- accent: NONE (restraint is the accent); at most one blue seal/logo
- motion: pure fade 0.4 s + 8 px rise; hold 3-4 s; no other motion AT ALL

**The grammar**: 「言葉を、読ませる。」 The viewer READS. Every other
element sacrifices itself. Narration = the copy, read plainly by the voice
the copy implies (a child for exam-season copy). Music stays under the
voice, never over.

## EffectCraft recipe

- Field: solid `#f7f5f0` + `Gradient Ramp` #ffffff→#efeadf diagonal.
- Copy: JP mincho (Noto Serif JP), fade 0.4 s + 8 px rise, ease-out 60.
- Narration: sttts-say, calm FEMALE/child-tone, verbatim copy, lines paced
  3-4 s apart.
- Logo: last 2 s, no glow.

## Anti-patterns

- Adding any effect, gradient orbs, glitch, or camera move.
- Reading narration ON the fade-in (voice enters after the text lands).
- More than 8 lines in 30 s.

## Best for

education, publishing, trust/craft messages, brand statements. GRAND2: fits
a「未来への一歩」brand-statement cut.
